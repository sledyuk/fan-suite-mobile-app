Rules

  1. The right column shows time above, one mark below. Never two marks.
  2. If the fan sent the last message: show the unread badge when unread, otherwise nothing.
  3. If you sent the last message: the preview starts with You: and the mark is exactly one of sending, delivered, seen, failed.
  4. The fan’s avatar on the right means seen by and appears only in the seen state.
  5. The presence dot on the big avatar is independent: online or offline, whatever the message state.
  6. Rows are ordered by the time of the last message, whether sent or received.

Assumption to confirm

The Figma frame shows two identical dots on every row and no times on the right, so “seen by” and “delivered” are our reading of it. Delivered as a single grey check is a proposal. Say the word and it becomes nothing at all, leaving only seen / sending / failed.
